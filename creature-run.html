THE WOODS

Keep Running

Shawn kept running.

He could hear his own breathing.

His shoes slammed against the dirt.

Branches scratched against his arms and face.

He wanted to stop.

Every part of him wanted to stop.

But something inside him told him to keep moving.

Don't look back.

So he didn't.

He ran toward where he thought the cabin was.

The trees seemed to stretch endlessly around him.

For a moment, he thought he could hear Karla.

"Shawn!"

He froze.

There was nothing.

Only the wind.

Then he heard it again.

"Shawn..."

It sounded like Karla.

But it was coming from behind him.

Shawn ran faster.

He didn't turn around.

He didn't answer.

Eventually, the trees began to thin.

Shawn saw the faint light of the cabin through the branches.

He stumbled forward.

He was almost home.

Almost safe.

But he couldn't stop thinking about the last time he heard Karla's voice.

He didn't know if she was still alive.

He didn't know what the creature had done to her.

And worst of all...

He didn't know if he could have saved her.

Run the rest of the way to the cabin.
