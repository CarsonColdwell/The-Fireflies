THE WOODS

The Fireflies

Shawn and Karla slipped between the trees.

The cabin disappeared behind them.

For a while, there was nothing unusual about the night.

Crickets chirped in the grass.

Leaves moved softly in the breeze.

Fireflies drifted through the darkness around them.

Karla laughed as one landed on her hand.

"Look!"

Shawn smiled.

"Don't crush it."

Karla carefully held her hand still.

The firefly crawled across her fingers before flying away.

"That was cool."

Shawn looked around at the trees.

They had wandered farther than he intended.

He turned toward where he thought the cabin was.

"We should head back."

Karla nodded.

Then the fireflies disappeared.

All of them.

At once.

Shawn stopped.

The woods had become completely quiet.

No crickets.

No wind.

Nothing.

Shawn looked toward the cabin.

He couldn't see it anymore.

Karla moved closer to him.

"Shawn?"

He didn't answer.

Somewhere in the darkness, a branch snapped.

Shawn grabbed Karla's hand.

Neither of them moved.

Another branch cracked.

This time, it was closer.

Run toward the cabin. Stay still and listen.
