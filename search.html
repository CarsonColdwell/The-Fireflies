THE WOODS — MORNING

The Search

Radimir was gone for hours.

Shawn stayed by the window.

He watched the darkness outside slowly turn gray.

Then gray became blue.

Then the first light of morning reached the trees.

Shawn hadn't slept.

He kept thinking about Karla.

He kept thinking about the thing between the trees.

And he kept thinking about the moment he ran.

Eventually, Shawn saw his father walking through the trees.

Radimir reached the cabin just after sunrise.

He looked exhausted.

There was mud on his boots.

His coat was torn at the shoulder.

Shawn ran to the door.

"Dad?"

Radimir stepped inside.

He set his rifle against the wall.

Shawn stared at him.

"Did you find her?"

Radimir didn't answer immediately.

"I searched every trail."

"The creek."

"The clearing."

"Every place I could think of."

Shawn waited.

"Did you find anything?"

Radimir looked at him.

"There isn't a trail."

Shawn frowned.

"What do you mean?"

"I mean there isn't a trail."

"No footprints."

"No blood."

"No broken branches."

Radimir paused.

"Nothing."

Shawn looked toward the window.

The woods were completely still.

Then something flickered between the trees.

One tiny light.

Then another.

Then another.

Fireflies.

Hundreds of them.

Shawn stepped closer to the window.

For just a moment, he thought he saw something standing between the trees.

Still.

Watching the cabin.

Shawn blinked.

There was nothing there.

Only the fireflies.

Look toward the fireflies.
