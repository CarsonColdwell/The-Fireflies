THE CABIN

Home

Shawn burst through the cabin door.

He slammed it shut behind him.

His hands were shaking.

He pushed the lock into place.

Then he stood there.

Breathing.

Listening.

Nothing followed him.

For a few seconds, Shawn thought he was safe.

Then he heard his father's voice.

"Shawn?"

Radimir stepped into the room.

He had been sitting at the table, cleaning one of his hunting knives.

He looked at Shawn.

Then he looked at the door.

"Where's Karla?"

Shawn opened his mouth.

Nothing came out.

"Where is your sister?"

Shawn finally spoke.

"We went outside."

Radimir's expression changed.

"You took her into the woods?"

Shawn nodded.

"We just wanted to see the fireflies."

His voice cracked.

"Something was there."

Radimir stood.

"What do you mean?"

"I don't know."

Shawn wiped his eyes.

"It was between the trees. It was—"

He stopped.

He couldn't find the words.

Radimir walked toward him.

"Where's Karla, Shawn?"

Shawn looked at the floor.

"I ran."

For the first time, Radimir looked afraid.

He grabbed his coat from the chair.

Then he took his rifle.

"Stay here."

Shawn looked up.

"Dad—"

"Stay here."

Radimir opened the door.

Cold air entered the cabin.

He stepped outside.

The door closed behind him.

Shawn was alone.

Wait for Dad to come back.
