THE HOLZMAN CABIN

The Fireflies

The Holzman cabin was small, but it was home.

Shawn had grown up in the woods surrounding it. His father had taught him how to follow tracks, where the best fishing spots were, and which trails were easiest to follow after dark.

Shawn knew these woods better than most people his age.

His little sister Karla didn't get to go with him and their father very often.

At eight years old, she was still too young to come along on their longer hunting trips.

She hated being left behind.

That evening, Shawn was getting ready for bed when Karla looked out the window.

Tiny lights flickered between the trees.

Fireflies.

Karla turned toward Shawn.

"Shawn, look."

She pointed toward the window.

"Can we go see them?"

Shawn looked outside.

The fireflies were everywhere.

He smiled.

It wouldn't take long.

"Come on. But we have to be quiet." "Dad said you're not supposed to go outside."
