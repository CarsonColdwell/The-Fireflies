THE HOLZMAN CABIN

The Cabin

Karla crossed her arms.

"Dad only said I'm not allowed to go hunting."

Shawn looked toward the other room.

Their father, Radimir, was sitting at the table cleaning one of his hunting knives.

Shawn looked back at Karla.

She was already putting on her shoes.

"We won't go far," she whispered.

Shawn sighed.

He knew they probably shouldn't.

But the fireflies were still glowing outside.

"Fine."

Karla smiled.

Sneak out through the back door. Stay inside and watch the fireflies through the window.
