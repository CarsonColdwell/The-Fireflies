THE WOODS

Look Back

Shawn stopped.

His chest burned.

He could still hear Karla crying somewhere behind him.

"Shawn!"

He closed his eyes.

He couldn't leave her.

Shawn turned around hesitantly.

Karla was just out of sight, hidden in darkness.

"Don't leave me, wait up."

She wasn't crying.

She was calm.

Too calm.

Shawn took a step toward her.

"Karla?"

She didn't answer.

Another step.

the sound of footseps could be heard coming from the darkness.

Something was wrong.

there were too many of them.

It wasn't her.

Then "she" spoke.

"Shawn."

It wasn't her voice.

Shawn turned and ran.

This time, he didn't wait.

He didn't look back.

He ran until he saw the cabin lights.

He ran until the trees were gone.

He ran until he could no longer hear anything behind him.

Run to the cabin.
